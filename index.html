The practice

Khensiwe PN is a design-and-build practice in Gauteng. We draw your home, render it so you can see it first, take it through council and your estate, then build it ourselves. One team, from the first peg in the ground to the final brick.

How a project runs

Five steps. One team.

  1. 01Brief & site visit

    We visit the stand or house, measure up and listen to what you want.

  2. 02Design & render

    Plans and 3D renders, refined until the design feels right.

  3. 03Approvals

    Submission to council and your estate committee where needed.

  4. 04Build

    Site preparation, structure and finishes, with regular progress updates.

  5. 05Handover

    Final walkthrough, snag list closed, keys in your hand.
